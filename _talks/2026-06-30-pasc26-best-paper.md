PASC26 Best Paper. The talk presents Jigsaw, a distributed training and inference scheme that combines domain and tensor parallelism. It was used to train WeatherMixer, an MLP-Mixer weather model with up to 3.2 billion parameters, reaching 11 PFLOPs on 256 GPUs with 72% scaling efficiency (compared with 51% without Jigsaw).
